# Promote a twice-amended rule surface to its own amendment skill

The general protocol names this obligation and leaves it unowned: "Use this skill directly only for a rule surface with no owner, and record that gap: a surface amended this way twice deserves its own skill" (`suggested/skills/amend-governing-rule-surface.md`, Step 1). That is the convergence-ladder trigger applied to amendments — two independent occurrences licensing a shared artifact — and per the workspace rule that a detectable gap class needs an owning artifact, the promotion procedure lands here.

## Step 1: Confirm the trigger from recorded history

The evidence is the two amendments' own records — each landed as "its own dated, attributed change" under the general protocol, so both are checkable by a third party (`suggested/agent-updates/run-history-is-the-amendment-evidence.md` sets the standard: recorded history, never the pressure of a current pass). Two amendments of the *same* surface (same registry cell type, same document genre) count; two different surfaces that happened to use the general skill do not. One amendment is what the general protocol already absorbs — do not promote speculatively.

## Step 2: Sweep for an existing owner

Search the per-surface skills by the surface's identity (what document, what cell, what defect shape), not by name — a skill that already owns this surface under other wording means the fix is a routing-table edit pointing at it, not a duplicate artifact.

## Step 3: Author the per-surface skill

Instantiate the general protocol the way the existing per-surface skills do (trigger tightening, sweep lightening): keep the invariant spine — defer out of the pressured moment, ground in recorded history, land as a dated attributed change — and add the surface-specific steps the two recorded amendments actually required (which cells change together, what conformance re-checks follow, who is notified). Quote both amendments as the grounding; a per-surface step neither amendment needed is speculation.

## Step 4: Repoint the route in the same change

Edit `suggested/docs/execution-defect-routing.md` so the surface's defect row names the new skill as owning fix artifact instead of the general protocol, in the same change the skill lands — the same-change coupling law. The general skill's recorded gap for this surface is closed by this change; note the closure where the gap was recorded.
